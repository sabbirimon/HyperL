## Work intentionally still pending

Full LLM/model sharding, general planner dispatch, durable recovery, production
tenant isolation, real TPU/NPU/new GPU adapters, enterprise fabric drivers, broad
precision execution, and live quantum-provider acceptance require their own
implementation and hardware gates. See [the prioritized agent backlog](AGENT_BACKLOG.md).
No throughput/speedup, hyperscaler scale, post-quantum security or provider availability
is inferred from interface support. Alpha.6 remains development source until published.
